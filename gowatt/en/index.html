Go Watt is an EV charging platform for Oman. This page walks through the whole journey, from signing in to a full battery. Nothing on this site is a live service: every booking, payment and map interface shown here illustrates what the app will do once it launches.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your phone number, email or Apple ID.
  2. Add your car once — model, plug type, battery capacity.
  3. Find the nearest station, ordered by distance and availability.
  4. Book a slot before you arrive.
  5. Plug in; charging starts and progress updates as it goes.
  6. The amount is taken from your in-app wallet.

Services

  • Mobile rescue van — a charging unit that comes to you.
  • Car-to-car charging — move energy between two vehicles.
  • Go Watt Ride — electric rides inside the same platform.
  • Home charger sharing — share your charger and earn from it.

Coverage

The plan starts with the main cities — Muscat, Sohar, Nizwa — then Salalah and Sur, then the roads between them. The locations shown on this site are illustrative, not a live network.

Points and membership

Every charge will earn Green Points redeemable against discounts, across three planned tiers: Standard, Silver and Gold.

FAQ

No. Those interfaces illustrate what the app will do.
